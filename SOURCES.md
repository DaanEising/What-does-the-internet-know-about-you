## Mandatory interpretation rules

- `S01` 102 minutes: across **Dutch ages 0–6** in 2026, not a claim about the 3-year-old character.
- `S02` 9.2 years: average among parent respondents whose children **already had a phone**, rather than average over every 7–12-year-old.
- `S03` 10 years and 3 months: **Flemish** parent reports from a filtered subset of respondents (chart n=583), not a Netherlands-specific age. It does not imply that every minor has an account.
- `S06` about 92,000 pupils: survey of students participating in MediaMasters and **not a population prevalence estimate**. Avoid turning this into “50% of all Dutch primary school kids”.
- 3, 9, 11, 14 and 18 are **illustrative character ages**. Age 14 posting and age 18 shopping are storytelling examples, not statistically established starts.
- Like / public-post first age: **no robust, comparable median established here**. Do not assign a sourced average or suggest universal social posting.
- Observed data could be gathered at an account, device, browser, or service level; do not assume it definitively identifies the child.
- Google / YouTube Kids documentation may change by age, product and user settings.
- Separate services may have distinct data sets; sharing or linking is not universal or automatic.
- Explicitly distinguish recommendations from targeting ads. The DSA prohibits ads based on profiling minors with reasonable certainty; it does **not** prohibit all personalization or all data processing.
- The sample running-interest inference is a **hypothetical example**, not evidence that a particular named provider drew that inference.
